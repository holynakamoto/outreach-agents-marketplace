# Product context — dave.io

## Product name & one-liner

**dave.io** — agentic engineering platform paired with a dedicated human SRE, delivering superior coverage at ~1/10th the cost of hiring a remote DevOps / Platform / SRE team.

## What it actually does (3 specific capabilities, not marketing)

1. **AI-driven engineering automation** — handles routine DevOps, Platform, and SRE workflows agentically (incidents, deploys, observability) so engineering teams don't need to scale headcount linearly with infrastructure complexity.
2. **Context & tribal-knowledge capture** — the AI persists institutional knowledge that normally walks out the door when engineers leave. Runbooks, decision rationale, and team conventions stay with the platform.
3. **Bundled dedicated human resource** — every engagement includes a real human SRE/engineer (the "local resource") who reviews AI-proposed changes before they touch production. Not a chatbot wrapper; a managed service with a name and a Slack handle.

## Anti-positioning — what it is NOT

- Not a pure AIOps tool you have to staff yourself (vs. PagerDuty AI, BigPanda)
- Not an offshore staff-aug agency that just sells human hours
- Not a chatbot or copilot — the human reviews and applies; nothing auto-runs against prod without sign-off
- Not trying to replace your senior engineering leadership — it replaces the cost/lifecycle of hiring 3–5 mid-level DevOps/Platform/SRE ICs

## Three "trigger events" that signal a hot account

1. **Active remote hiring for DevOps / Platform / SRE** — 2+ open reqs in the last 60 days. Strongest single signal. Implies budget already approved, capability gap acknowledged.
2. **Recent funding round (Series A/B/C in last 6 months)** — fresh runway, pressure from board to scale efficiently without ballooning headcount. CFOs especially receptive.
3. **Public reliability events** — visible status page incidents, post-mortems shared on engineering blog, or customer-facing SLA misses. The COO knows about these and is being asked what's being done.

## Banned words & phrases for outbound

These signal AI-generated mail to engineering and executive buyers. Hard rule: never appear in cold outreach.

- transform, leverage, synergy, holistic, game-changing, revolutionize, unlock, AI-powered, cutting-edge, best-in-class, world-class
- "I hope this email finds you well" / "I wanted to reach out" / "I came across your profile" / "I noticed you're the..."
- "Quick question" (overused to the point of meaninglessness)
- Any sentence starting with "As a [their title], you..."
- Em-dashes between every clause (signals LLM output)

## Notes for the Personalization Agent specifically

When drafting outreach for this product:

- **Lead with the hiring signal.** If they have 3 open DevOps reqs, say so in the first sentence with a specific link or job title. Don't say "I noticed you're hiring" — say "Your DevOps Engineer (US/Remote) posting from 5/2 caught my eye."
- **For CFOs:** quantify the alternative cost. "$600K in loaded comp" beats "save money."
- **For CTOs:** lean on tribal-knowledge capture and attrition risk. They've all lost a senior IC and felt the pain.
- **For COOs:** lead with reliability + named accountability. "A human SRE you can Slack" is concrete; "AI-powered" is abstract.
- **Avoid the word "cheaper."** Use "1/10th the cost" or specific dollar deltas — frames as a structural change, not a discount.
